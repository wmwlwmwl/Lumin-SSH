# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and operations engineers using a desktop SSH client to manage remote systems, files, terminals, automation, and AI-assisted workflows across Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## Product Purpose

Lumin provides a fast, low-friction desktop workspace for SSH and local terminal work without requiring a server-side agent. Success means users can connect, inspect, transfer, troubleshoot, and use AI assistance without leaving their operational flow.

## Brand Personality

Efficient, restrained, dependable. The interface should feel like a focused professional tool and preserve the established Lumin interaction vocabulary.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative redesigns, novelty controls, consumer-chat styling, excessive animation, and visual changes that make dense operational workflows less familiar or slower to scan.

## Design Principles

- Keep the terminal and active task primary.
- Add capability through familiar, compact controls.
- Make persistence, destructive actions, and security boundaries explicit.
- Preserve consistency across panels, settings, and themes.
- Prefer immediate feedback without interrupting the user's flow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, non-color-only state communication, and reduced-motion compatibility across supported themes and locales.
